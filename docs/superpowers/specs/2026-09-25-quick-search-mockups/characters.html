Round 2: which character should pop up?

Seven characters, each with its own look, props, and moves. In the app each one would also react: sleepy when waiting, excited when it finds your file, a little shrug when nothing matches. Click your favourite, or tell me in the terminal (for example "the owl, but with Sparky's glow").

A · Sparky

DeskAI's logo spark, come to life, with three tiny stars orbiting it. Glows brighter the harder it searches, and waves hello.

B · Archie the owl

A wise little librarian in round glasses. Its eyes dart left and right while it looks, and it hands you a file card with what it found.

C · Pip the robot

A tiny hover-robot with a glowing screen face, DeskAI's spark on its chest, and a magnifying glass. Its jet flickers while it searches.

D · Fetch the fox

A fox in a mint scarf that fetches your file and brings it back in its mouth. Tail wags, ears twitch, scarf flutters.

E · Inky the octopus

Grabs lots of files at once with its arms: a photo in one, a PDF in another. Blows bubbles and winks while it searches.

F · Mochi

A jelly blob with a little leaf sprout, sparkly eyes and a gold star in its hand. Squishes, bounces, and its sprout sways.

G · Paige the paper ghost

A friendly ghost made of notebook paper, with a paperclip bow. It floats through your folders and says "boo!" when it finds your file.
